# Changelog

The first line under each version is the summary shown when Eardloom offers an update.

## 0.1.3

Share your whole world, and format your writing.

- **Share world** (from the world's home page): export a **wiki website** anyone can open in a browser, with entry pages, categories, links, backlinks and search. Upload the folder to Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages to share a link. Private entries are never included, and spoilers sit behind a Reveal button.
- **World packs**: save a whole world as one `.eardloom` file for another Eardloom user, or as a backup. Open one from the home screen.
- **Formatting toolbar** on articles and manuscripts: headings, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, lists, quotes and scene breaks.

## 0.1.2

Updates now announce themselves, and you can see what changed.

- When a new version is out, Eardloom tells you as soon as it opens, with the release notes and a one-click update. Choose Later and a reminder stays at the top of the window.
- After updating, a **What's new** window shows everything that changed since your last version. You can reopen it from Settings.
- Eardloom also checks for updates every few hours while it's open.

## 0.1.1

Export your books, share entries as images, and give entries an icon.

- **Book export** to EPUB, Word and Markdown. Pick which chapters to include, and turn entry links into plain text for readers.
- **Share as image**: turn any entry into a portrait or banner card, in a dark or light style, to save or paste into a chat.
- **Entry icons**: give an entry an emoji, shown in lists wherever it has no image.
- **Default author name** in Settings, used for every export unless a book overrides it.

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Worlds with your own categories and fields, built from genre starter templates.
- Linked entries with backlinks, an infobox and full-text search.
- Writing projects with a binder and a lore panel beside the manuscript.
- In-app updates.

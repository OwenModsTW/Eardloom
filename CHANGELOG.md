# Changelog

The first line under each version is the summary shown when Eardloom offers an update.

## 0.1.9

Entry history, side-by-side writing, family trees, images, tables and footnotes, and much more.

- **Lore with your assistant**: the assistant can now write and rewrite full articles for your entries, with headings and lists, and every change can be undone. Your books stay yours: it gives feedback on your story but won't write it.
- **Entry history**: entries keep earlier versions, automatically before your first edit each session and before any assistant or bulk change, and whenever you save one. Compare any version with now and restore it. Open History on any entry.
- **Side by side**: open an entry, another chapter or a map beside the chapter you're writing. Drag to resize; it's remembered for each book.
- **Read as one**: click a folder, or the book button, to read and edit all its chapters as one continuous document.
- **Relationships**: give link fields a name for the other direction (a planet's "Homeworld" shows as "Native peoples" on the planet), see them under Connections on each entry, and hover the graph to see what each connection is.
- **Family trees**: mark link fields as Parents, Children, Partners or Siblings to see a family tree for any character.
- **Templates and bulk changes**: save an entry as a template and start new ones from it. Tick entries in a category's table to tag, move, change visibility or delete them together.
- **Images, tables and footnotes** in chapters and articles, included when you export to EPUB, Word or a website.
- **Page view** shows your chapter as paper with page breaks marked, and the footer counts manuscript pages.
- **Find in book** (Ctrl+Shift+F) searches every chapter, and can replace across them all, taking a snapshot first.
- **Fixes**: the assistant's changes to the entry you have open now show straight away instead of being overwritten, replies display headings and lists properly, and long list values no longer overlap.

This update upgrades your worlds' storage. After updating, older versions of Eardloom can't open them.

## 0.1.8

The assistant remembers what you talked about in each entry and chapter.

- **A conversation for every entry and chapter**: open an entry and the assistant picks up where you left off, even after closing Eardloom. Each entry and chapter keeps its own conversation, and there's one about your world as a whole for everywhere else. Suggestions you hadn't decided on are still waiting for you. Start any conversation over with the ↺ button. Conversations are kept in your world and its backups, but never included when you share it.
- **Better entry suggestions**: the assistant now writes descriptions, history and detail into the entry's article and keeps the summary to a sentence or two.
- **Tidier replies**: headings, numbered lists and dividers in the assistant's replies now display properly, and text no longer runs under the scrollbar.
- **Fix**: long values in an entry's list fields no longer overlap each other.

This update upgrades your worlds' storage. After updating, older versions of Eardloom can't open them.

## 0.1.7

A built-in writing assistant that runs entirely on your computer, if you want it.

- **Built-in assistant** (off until you turn it on in Settings): chat about your world without any account or subscription. It runs on your own computer, using your graphics card when it has one, and your conversations never leave it. Choose a model to suit your hardware; Eardloom recommends one and downloads it once. Like the connected assistants, it can look things up, review chapters and suggest entries, comments and small line edits for you to approve, but it won't write your book. It starts when you use it, stops after ten minutes idle, and always closes with Eardloom. Open it from the sidebar or with Ctrl+J.
- **Connecting other AI apps**: one-click Connect for Claude Code, and Eardloom now asks you to quit Claude Desktop before connecting it, so the change isn't lost.
- **About**: Settings now shows the version, a privacy statement, and the licences of the open-source projects Eardloom is built with.

## 0.1.6

Maps, a graph of your world, planning views, comments, backups, and AI assistants that help without writing for you.

- **Maps**: draw your own (paint land, forests, lakes, regions, rivers, roads, routes and borders, stamp symbols, add labels) in Parchment, Atlas or Starchart style, generate a coastline to start from, or bring in any image. Pin entries onto any map, link pins to other maps, and export as PNG. Entries show which maps they're on.
- **Graph**: see your whole world as a web of connections, filter by category, include chapters, and find anything. Every entry also shows a small graph of what it's connected to.
- **Cards and Outline**: plan a book on index cards (drag to reorder) or in an outline table, with statuses and colour labels you can customise per book.
- **Comments**: select text and leave yourself a note in the margin. Resolve them when done; they never appear in exports.
- **Backups**: your worlds are backed up automatically, without filling your disk. Nothing is saved if nothing changed, older backups are thinned out, and each world has a size limit. Restore any backup as a copy from Settings.
- **Spelling**: Eardloom now has its own spellchecker (UK or US English). Right-click a word to **Learn** it for this world, and every entry name counts as spelt correctly.
- **AI assistants**: connect your own Claude or Codex app in Settings and ask it about your world. It can look things up, review your chapters and suggest new entries, comments and small line edits, which you approve or skip in Eardloom. It's there to help you write, not to write for you.

This update upgrades your worlds' storage. After updating, older versions of Eardloom can't open them.

## 0.1.5

Snapshots of your chapters, and pasted text keeps its formatting.

- **Snapshots**: save a version of any chapter (Ctrl+S, or the history button above the page), compare it word by word with what you have now, and restore it if you change your mind. Eardloom also keeps a snapshot automatically the first time you edit a chapter in each session.
- **Pasting from other writing apps** that copy rich text now keeps italics, bold, underline, strikethrough, superscript, alignment and headings, instead of pasting plain text.

## 0.1.4

A much more capable writing editor.

- **More formatting**: alignment, highlight and text colours, superscript and subscript, links, list indenting and clear formatting. Curly quotes, em-dashes and ellipses are typed for you.
- **Find and replace** in any document (Ctrl+F, Ctrl+H), with match case and whole words.
- **Live counts**: words, characters, the words in your selection and reading time, plus **word targets** for each chapter and each book, and how much you've written this session.
- **Focus mode** (F11): full screen with nothing but the page. Optional typewriter scrolling keeps the line you're writing in place, and you can fade the other paragraphs.
- **Page appearance**: choose the manuscript font, size, line spacing and page width, and switch to novel-style paragraph indents.
- **Spellcheck** while you write, with suggestions on right-click.
- Exports keep the new formatting.

## 0.1.3

A timeline for your world, a new home page, and sharing your whole world.

- **Timeline**: give your world its own calendar (eras, months, any way of counting years), add **Date on the timeline** fields to any category (an event's date, a character's birth, a span like a war or a reign), and see everything in order on the new Timeline page.
- **A new world home page**: a cover image, pick up the chapter you were writing, pinned entries, your codex at a glance, the latest on your timeline, and a to-do list of entries that need a summary or that nothing links to yet.
- **Create entries as you write**: type @ and a name that doesn't exist yet, choose a category, and it's created and linked in one go.
- **Share world** (from the world's home page): export a **wiki website** anyone can open in a browser, with entry pages, categories, links, backlinks and search. Upload the folder to Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages to share a link. Private entries are never included, and spoilers sit behind a Reveal button.
- **World packs**: save a whole world as one `.eardloom` file for another Eardloom user, or as a backup. Open one from the home screen.
- **Formatting toolbar** on articles and manuscripts: headings, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, lists, quotes and scene breaks.

## 0.1.2

Updates now announce themselves, and you can see what changed.

- When a new version is out, Eardloom tells you as soon as it opens, with the release notes and a one-click update. Choose Later and a reminder stays at the top of the window.
- After updating, a **What's new** window shows everything that changed since your last version. You can reopen it from Settings.
- Eardloom also checks for updates every few hours while it's open.

## 0.1.1

Export your books, share entries as images, and give entries an icon.

- **Book export** to EPUB, Word and Markdown. Pick which chapters to include, and turn entry links into plain text for readers.
- **Share as image**: turn any entry into a portrait or banner card, in a dark or light style, to save or paste into a chat.
- **Entry icons**: give an entry an emoji, shown in lists wherever it has no image.
- **Default author name** in Settings, used for every export unless a book overrides it.
